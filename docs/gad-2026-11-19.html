BMC gastroenterology · Role of small intestinal bacteria overgrowth in irritable bowel syndrome and anxiety: a retrospective Electronic Health Record (EHR)- based cohort study using triNetX.

巴勒斯坦高等教育中的冒充者現象：與自尊、焦慮及競爭力的關聯－橫斷面研究

BMC psychology · The impostor phenomenon in Palestinian higher education: associations with self-esteem, anxiety, and competitiveness - a cross-sectional study.
